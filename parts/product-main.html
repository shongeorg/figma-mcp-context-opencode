The Barberton Daisy (Gerbera jamesonii) is one of the most rewarding houseplants you can grow. Native to the rocky slopes of Mpumalanga it enjoys a bright position a metre away from a south-facing window, a steady 18–24 °C and a substrate that dries out slightly between waterings.

Light

Bright, indirect light keeps the foliage compact and produces the strongest flower colour. Direct midday sun can scorch the leaves, while too little light will stop the plant flowering altogether.

Watering & care

Water thoroughly and let the excess drain, then wait until the top two centimetres of compost are dry. Remove spent flowers at the base of the stem to encourage new buds, and feed with a high-potash fertiliser every two weeks while the plant is in bloom.

Every plant is shipped in a nursery pot so it can be potted on immediately, and each order is packed by hand in our Farmingdale greenhouse just before despatch.

Temperature & humidity

Keep the plant above 15 °C and away from cold draughts, radiators and frosted windows. Average household humidity is perfect; in a very dry room stand the pot on a tray of damp pebbles and top up the water as it evaporates.

Pet safety

Gerbera species are listed as non-toxic to cats and dogs by the ASPCA, which makes them a good choice for homes with curious pets. As with any houseplant the compost should not be eaten and watering should always be done from the top of the pot.
